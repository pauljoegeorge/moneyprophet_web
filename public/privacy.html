Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy covers the Money Prophet iPhone app and website, operated by Paul Joe George.

Information we collect and why

  • Account information: your name, email address (including an Apple private relay address when provided), provider account identifier, and session tokens, used to authenticate and manage your account.
  • Financial and app content: expense amounts, currencies, dates, categories, notes, budgets, recurring bills, rewards, and generated reports, used to provide your expense tracker and reports.
  • Optional web location: when you have granted browser permission, website expense entry can attach coordinates to an expense. Entry still works without location. The iPhone app does not fetch or attach location.
  • Operational records: requests may create server logs including IP addresses, request times, response statuses, and errors, used to operate and protect the service. Do not put passwords, bank credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information in expense notes.

Sign-in and service providers

Google and Apple process sign-in through their own services. Money Prophet receives the account information you authorize them to share. Hosting providers process account and app data to operate the service. We do not sell your personal data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

AI features and your permission

Before using AI features, you can review and allow AI data sharing. Without permission, manual entry and ordinary spending reports remain available.

  • AI quick entry sends your entered text and category names to Google Gemini.
  • AI insights and forecasts send expense amounts, dates, notes, categories, budgets, and recent expense history to Google Gemini.
  • Web receipt entry sends receipt content to Google Cloud Vision for text recognition and Google Gemini for expense extraction. Receipt capture is not offered in the iPhone app.

These providers process this information to return the requested result, under their applicable service terms and privacy practices. See Google’s privacy policy. Do not include bank passwords or other secrets in AI input. We do not intentionally send your sign-in tokens or account email in AI prompts.

Withdraw AI permission at any time in the iPhone app’s Account & privacy settings or the website’s AI privacy settings. Withdrawal prevents new AI requests; it does not recall information already processed or cancel a request already underway. Stored AI reports are removed with your account.

Storage and security

Account and financial data are stored on the service. The iPhone app keeps session credentials in iOS Keychain and may keep drafts, chat history, and preferences on your device. Web sessions use browser storage. We take measures to protect data, but no storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and financial records while your account remains active. In the iPhone app, open Account & privacy and select Delete account to permanently delete your account and associated active-service records. Deletion also removes local drafts and sessions from that installation and revokes the linked Apple authorization. Information already sent to service providers is governed by their retention practices; backup and operational records may remain subject to their retention and applicable legal requirements.

You may request access, correction, or deletion assistance through our operator’s website. You can edit expenses and account preferences in Money Prophet.

Changes and contact

We will publish policy changes here with an updated effective date. Material changes to AI sharing require renewed permission. For privacy questions, contact the operator through www.paulworks.online.
